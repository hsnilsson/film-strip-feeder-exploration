## Shared learnings

- **Film travel and frame position.** Counting perforations or moving by a fixed step can index regularly spaced frames. Detecting frame boundaries in the image can align exposures with uneven spacing. Test a loose single frame separately, where the cut edges may be the only boundaries. [Mongoose modes](https://www.cameradactyl.com/buttergrip/p/mongoose) · [TechcakeLab perforation counting](https://github.com/techcakelab/Auto-film-scanning) · [FruitieX frame detection](https://github.com/FruitieX/negative_scanner#automatic)
- **Reuse a carrier to test the drive.** Sigí and Isik both fitted motorized advance to an existing carrier. That gives us a way to test drive and positioning before building a new gate. [Sigí prototype](https://sigi-scan.com/) · [Isik build](https://petapixel.com/2020/09/23/how-to-make-an-auto-35mm-film-scanner-with-arduino-nano-and-python/)

The surveyed builds show several ways to move film through a carrier. Unattended feeding from a stack of loose negatives, including single frames, remains undocumented in these builds. [Gretag Imaging's published stack pickup design](docs/2-pickup-and-separation.md#closest-published-design-for-loose-strips)
