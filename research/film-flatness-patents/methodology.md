# Research method and coverage

## Discovery and selection

The search started from [US5028956A](patents/US5028956A.md) and [EP0660170A1](patents/EP0660170A1.md). It followed original references backwards to glassless holders, optical printers and film-tensioning systems, and followed forward references to multiformat clamps, segment handling and later tensioning devices. Searches combined photography, aerial cameras, cinematography, enlargers, minilabs, scanners and microfilm with descriptions such as `film gate`, `pressure pad`, `negative carrier`, `platen`, `vacuum hold-down`, `copy master`, `curl`, `buckle` and `lateral tension`. Dates did not determine eligibility.

Classification browsing and comparisons used CPC G03B1/42 and G03B1/48 for film guidance and flattening around an exposure plane, G03B27/52 and G03B27/62 for handling originals in printing apparatus, and H04N1/10 for scanner transport. Related transport terms included B65H tensioning and web guidance. [Official G03B classification scheme](https://www.cooperativepatentclassification.org/sites/default/files/cpc/scheme/G/scheme-G03B.pdf)

Google Patents provided searchable indexes, family links, reference discovery and facsimiles. The European Publication Server provided the EPO original of EP0660170A1. A direct Espacenet lookup returned HTTP 403 to the research client. Espacenet links are provided on the records for independent family searches. [EPO Publication Server](https://www.epo.org/en/searching-for-patents/technical/publication-server) · [Espacenet patent-number search](https://worldwide.espacenet.com/patent/help/query-syntax-patent-numbers)

## Records and drawings

The catalog documents 47 selected publications from 1890 to 2024, with 47 complete facsimiles and 82 cropped drawing sheets. A related publication from the same family can be an additional reading source without adding another record. The dates identify first indexed priority, filing in the selected jurisdiction and publication of the selected document. Where an early US grant scan records a filing date without claiming an earlier one, the catalog uses the filing date for priority. Original assignee names can vary across country records and machine indexes.

The PDFs are complete facsimiles. PNGs are crops of original drawing sheets with their figure labels. The caption identifies the publication and one-based PDF page. Page numbers refer to the local facsimile, including front matter. Technical descriptions paraphrase the patent. Discussion of trade-offs and camera-scanning relevance is engineering interpretation. Evidence that a patent exists, or even reports testing, establishes neither the sale of a product nor its use of the stated mechanism.

## Remaining gaps

This is a selected archive. The search is strongest for English-language and US-indexed patents and two Kodak citation chains. German and Japanese documents were surfaced, but untranslated and hard-to-retrieve sources were examined less deeply. Additional work could close gaps in nineteenth-century plate and roll-film holders, non-US optical-printer patents, commercial microfilm equipment and later film scanners. Manufacturer manuals, parts diagrams and documented teardown evidence are needed to establish model-level commercial use. Untested issues include friction at aged emulsion, corner height on individual frames, performance after repeated cycles and mixed curl.
