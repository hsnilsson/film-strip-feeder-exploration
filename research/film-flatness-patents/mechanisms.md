# Mechanical principles

Transverse means across the strip, from one long edge to the other. Longitudinal means in the feed direction, from one cut end toward the other. Normal force is perpendicular to the film surface. Keeping these directions separate helps distinguish grip, positioning and flattening.

| Principle | What establishes the reference | Drawings to compare |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mechanical clamping | Opposing pieces press the margins or a surrounding frame against a reference surface. | [Oscillating leaf](patents/US2063016A.md), [opposed flaps](patents/US4483612A.md) |
| Edge grip | Rails, line contacts or perforation pins constrain selected margins. Edge grip can register, feed or transmit tension. | [Spring platen](patents/US2168688A.md), [registered optical printer](patents/US4835555A.md) |
| Vacuum | A pressure differential pulls the film onto a support. Port position, flow and seals govern where the pressure acts. | [Duplex vacuum holder](patents/US3237543A.md), [stepping microfilm camera](patents/US3966320A.md) |
| Glass | Transparent plates take the reference plane across the image. Opening before travel can reduce drag. | [Interlocked reader](patents/US2008982A.md), [microfilm cassette](patents/US4203658A.md) |
| Lateral tension | Opposite long margins are held and one or both move outward. | [Toggle carrier](patents/US2824491A.md), [belt and clamp](patents/US5028956A.md) |
| Longitudinal tension | A feed-side and take-up-side element oppose one another or a four-sided clamp pulls the ends outward. | [Sprocket-driven printer](patents/US2227987A.md), [gimballed scanner gate](patents/US5258807A.md) |
| Controlled bending | Shaped slots, a radiused guide or a deliberately curved support directs the film where its stiffness helps control its shape. | [Angled sheet margins](patents/US1139079A.md), [rolling scanner gate](patents/US5088813A.md) |
| Pneumatic pressure and air bearings | Gas flow applies a load or cushions the film around the optical access. | [Bernoulli scan gate](patents/US5111241A.md), [opposed air bearings](patents/US6020952A.md) |
| Thermal control | Illumination influences temperature gradients and the rate of film buckling. | [Projector edge illumination](patents/US6501530B2.md) |
| Combined mechanisms | Different components address different axes, contact conditions or times in the feed cycle. | [Scanning gate with end clamps](patents/EP0660170A1.md), [tow-web gate](patents/US5406352A.md) |

## Compare the free ends

For a single loose frame, a design may need to acquire a curled leading edge before the second contact is available. The film may still retain end curl even when transverse bow is reduced. Existing concepts for two synchronized edge belts and outward displacement are hypotheses. Compare them with pivoting flaps, gentle reverse bending, an exposure-only transparent reference and asymmetric edge registration. Evaluate center and corner height, repeatability after release, contact marks, scratch risk while moving and the ability to reacquire the shortest samples.
