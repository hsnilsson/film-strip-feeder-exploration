# Mechanism views

Read the film contact and motion alongside original figures from the archive. Numbered annotations pick out the relevant parts of each drawing. Select a figure to enlarge the complete sheet or open the cited page of the patent.

Each patent has a rotatable reconstruction and a figure guide covering the drawing sheets in its original PDF. Choose a numbered figure for its source plate and the corresponding viewpoint or configuration. Circuit diagrams and timing charts link to the subsystem they describe. Model geometry remains schematic wherever the patent leaves measurements unspecified.

[Hoffmaster's holder](../patents/US2824491A.md#patent-model) shows why the two end toggles and the long gripping bars move together. [Kodak's belt clamp](../patents/US5028956A.md#patent-model) shows how the belt runs along the film and how the cylinder moves across it during clamping.
