# Mechanism views

Read the film contact and motion alongside original figures from the archive. Numbered annotations pick out the relevant parts of each drawing. Select a figure to enlarge the complete sheet or open the cited page of the patent.

[Hoffmaster's rotatable holder](../patents/US2824491A.md#hoffmaster-model) shows why the two end toggles and the long gripping bars move together. [Kodak's belt clamp](../patents/US5028956A.md#mechanism-views) uses paired sections to show where the belt and cylinder contact the film and in which order. The 3D view and redraws show the relationships and documented states. Geometry without a published dimension remains schematic.
