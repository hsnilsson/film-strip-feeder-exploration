# From film packs to batch negative feeders

## Separate one by giving it a different edge

Clarke's film pack changed the consumable itself. Two notch patterns alternate through the stack. Stops line up with the exposed sheet's notches to release it while retaining the next sheet. Another photographic pack used paired leaders and connector strips to withdraw a photosensitive sheet with its matching print receiver and to stage the next leader. Both designs relied on deliberately prepared media. [Clarke's notched films](patents/US571470A.md) · [Agfa's coupled film pack](patents/US3650751A.md)

## Isolate a corner before moving the whole sheet

Vacuum pickup can lift a flexible sheet at a localized place. A shaped suction head, moving cup, corner stop or air flow can open an interface between the selected sheet and the next one. X-ray film loaders combine the lift with provisions for dark handling and a controlled release into rollers. Those machines show how to sequence the pickup, release and nip. Their large, comparatively uniform sheets give the separator more edge length and a different bending response than a clipped 35 mm negative. [Film-sheet suction device](patents/US4979729A.md) · [Bending and air in a cassette](patents/EP0512084B1.md)

Kodak's DryView 8100 service manual documents spring-loaded suction cups, a heel pad and separator tabs that hold back film at maximum bend. Its diagnosis of sheets slipping past the tabs supplies product-level evidence for that separation principle. The manual predates the later patents' silicone-loop and controlled-vibration variants, whose commercial use remains unresolved. [Kodak DryView 8100 service manual, section 7-6-5](https://www.scribd.com/document/522250501/sm8100d) · [Silicone-loop supply system](patents/WO2004079448A1.md) · [Controlled-vibration patent](patents/WO2007019045A1.md)

Document feeders add a second kind of evidence. Retard pads or rollers control relative motion when two sheets reach the throat together. Air-knife and vacuum-belt systems use pressure and shape to keep the next sheet behind. Ultrasonic sensing can detect an overlap that survives the mechanical stage. Contact pressure, friction and bending over a short span are specific to the media. [Active retard roller](patents/US6932338B1.md) · [Vacuum corrugation feeder](patents/US4627605A.md) · [Ultrasonic double-feed detection](patents/US7654522B2.md)

## Lab reorders and digitally scanned negatives

Gretag's reorder system places a hopper ahead of its existing strip track. A spring-biased plate squares the stack, a vacuum cup pivots one strip toward the exit and a movable pinch roller closes the nip. An infrared sensor reports whether material is available at the hopper. Tenbrock's later loader addresses irregularly cut and curled processed negatives explicitly. It proposes lifting the top strip with a vacuum head, loosening its neighbor with air and passing the lifted strip to registered drive. The patents describe designs for cut film, with the underlying tests and commercially deployed versions still open. [Gretag reorder feeder](patents/US6556276B2.md) · [Tenbrock's batch loader](patents/US7950654B2.md)

Mounted-slide feeders solve a related lab problem with a different set of contact surfaces. Their rigid frames give a pusher or separator a length and depth to bear on. A design that works on the frame may need an entirely different retention or handoff geometry for loose film. [Kodak automatic slide hopper](patents/US5555042A.md) · [Multi-thickness slide stack](patents/EP1267203B1.md)

## PowerFilm Plus as a commercial comparison

Pacific Image specifies batches of up to ten standard 35 mm strips, each two to six frames long. This is evidence for a commercial batch feeder in that length band. The manual shows the insertion guides and roller access. The project photos and feeding footage show the angled entrance, rollers and two felt-like supports. The idea that those supports push the dwindling stack into the pickup roller remains the project's interpretation. A patent-to-product match for that mechanism remains unverified.
