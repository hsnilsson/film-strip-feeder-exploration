# Search, evidence and coverage

## Discovery

The search began with [Gretag's multiple-strip feeder](patents/US6556276B2.md), then followed references and alternate terms for negative strips, sheet-film handling, separation at a magazine exit, batch slide feeding, vacuum peel, air knives, retard rollers and double-feed sensing. It considered photographic and X-ray equipment first and used sheet/document feeders as mechanical analogues. Publication date was an observed property of the sources.

Classification passes used B65H3/00 and its descendants for separating articles from stacks, including friction and pneumatic subclasses. B65H7/00 and neighboring intake-sensing groups led to overlap detection. Photography searches used G03B film-handling classes and neighboring G03D and H04N indexing for lab work and scanners. Backward and forward citations supply additional leads and family context. [Official B65H classification scheme](https://www.cooperativepatentclassification.org/sites/default/files/cpc/scheme/B/scheme-B65H.pdf) · [Official G03B classification scheme](https://www.cooperativepatentclassification.org/sites/default/files/cpc/scheme/G/scheme-G03B.pdf)

The searchable patent records come from Google Patents. Where available, European original PDFs come from the EPO Publication Server. Links to Espacenet are supplied for each publication. Direct Espacenet lookup was inaccessible in the research client, so the associated family evidence available from the publication and its searchable patent record was used. [EPO Publication Server](https://www.epo.org/en/searching-for-patents/technical/publication-server)

## Selection and source handling

The catalog contains 32 representative publications from 1896 to 2023, with 32 complete PDFs and 64 cropped original drawing sheets. The unit of selection is a representative publication for a distinct mechanism or a closely relevant detector. Closely related grants and application publications can be listed without a second facsimile. Pure strip advance after acquisition was kept out of the catalog unless the publication also explains the transfer from the separator or a significant alternate approach.

Each saved PDF is the complete publication. Drawing images are cropped from one-based PDF pages, with the original figure labels retained. Dates refer to the selected publication, earliest indexed priority and its filing. Where a nineteenth-century record required a date or name transcribed from a facsimile, its record identifies the basis. Related entries include mechanical comparisons as well as documented citation links.

Commercial notes distinguish manufacturer evidence, independently documented physical examples, patent-family relations and unresolved product identity. A device can establish that a mechanism category was used without proving that a specific patent embodiment shipped. Evidence about a mounted slide or a large X-ray sheet cannot establish the minimum length for unmounted 35 mm negatives.

Status links concern the recorded jurisdiction. Dated events, index-only status and ordinary-term estimates are identified in the catalog. PCT publications link to the international application and use Unknown for national or regional rights. [PCT filing and national phases](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/pct-system/introduction)

## Gaps

The evidence is strongest for the disclosed operating sequences and weakest for commercial adoption of particular embodiments. Additional model-level service manuals, parts lists and dated teardown photographs would improve that comparison. Coverage is weighted toward English-searchable publications and EPO facsimiles. Arcuate curl, static, adhesion, damaged perforations and abrasive contact remain test variables. The current sources leave the reliable one-frame boundary open for loose cut strips.
