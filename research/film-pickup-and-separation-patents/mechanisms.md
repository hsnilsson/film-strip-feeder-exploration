# Pickup, separation and handoff mechanisms

Pickup establishes contact with the selected piece. Separation opens a gap or holds the next piece back while the selected one leaves. Handoff transfers control to the receiving nip, sprocket or carrier. A useful comparison follows all three actions through the same cycle.

| Principle | What selects one piece | Drawings to compare |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Friction pickup | A roller, belt or pad advances the face presented by the stack. A gate, bend or retard element supplies an opposing reaction. | [X-ray film throat](patents/EP0521459A1.md) · [Calibrated withdrawal slot](patents/US4365793A.md) |
| Retard pad or roller | Friction at the second surface opposes movement of the following sheet. A driven retard roller can reverse when the feeder encounters an overlap. | [Toggling pad](patents/US3966189A.md) · [Reversible retard roller](patents/US6932338B1.md) |
| Vacuum pickup | Pressure difference retains the exposed face as a cup, tube or belt moves the selected sheet. | [Gretag pivoting pickup](patents/US6556276B2.md) · [X-ray film suction device](patents/US4979729A.md) |
| Air separation | A jet opens the interface as a cup or belt captures the selected sheet. | [Tenbrock's cut negative loader](patents/US7950654B2.md) · [Front air knife](patents/US4627605A.md) |
| Bending and peeling | Asymmetric lift, a concave profile or a retained edge makes the selected sheet bend away from the next. | [X-ray cassette removal](patents/EP0512084B1.md) · [Claw and edge deformation](patents/US3955092A.md) |
| Mechanical grip or selection gate | A pusher, claw, stop or keyed edge selects the exposed element. Holdback can come from the next layer's geometry. | [Alternating film notches](patents/US571470A.md) · [Slide thickness separator](patents/EP1267203B1.md) |
| Detection | A presence sensor can trigger pickup. An independent thickness or ultrasonic measurement can look for overlap before the receiver accepts the piece. | [Gretag's hopper presence sensor](patents/US6556276B2.md) · [Ultrasonic double-feed detector](patents/US7654522B2.md) |

## Compare complete handoffs

Use cut 35 mm film in mixed lengths. Include free single frames, short strips of several frames and longer cut strips. Vary transverse curl, lengthwise curl, stack height, cut-end condition, surface and orientation. Record the length at which the next transport first takes grip and the distance available before the picker lets go.

Count misses, doubles, skew, jams and surface marks after the same handling cycle. Use inexpensive sacrificial film first and inspect the departing piece and its neighbor. A whole-roll feeder can be evaluated later as a separate input to the same receiving point. [Project pickup trials](../../docs/2-pickup-and-separation.md#open-questions-and-experiments)
